Connect your European Zeekr to Homey Pro for battery, range, charging, climate and other reported vehicle states. Request supported cloud controls for locks, windows, lids, heating, ventilation, charging limits and schedules through device buttons and Flows. English and Swedish are supported. Requires your own Zeekr EU account and a complete valid API key file; Home Assistant is not required.

Unofficial community integration, shared as-is without a commitment to maintenance or support. New controls are mock-tested, not verified against a real vehicle by this port. Support depends on model, region, firmware and account. An accepted request does not prove a performed action. No Bluetooth or digital driving key is included.

Original API work: Fryyyyy. Key tools: Wysie. MIT notices preserved. Downloads and instructions: https://github.com/homeyrglabs/Zeekr-Homey/releases
