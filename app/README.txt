Connect your European Zeekr to Homey Pro to follow the battery level, range and charging status, and request charging or cabin climate through Flows. Requires a Zeekr EU account and a complete valid API key file, which you must extract separately. Home Assistant is not required.

Unofficial community integration, shared as-is without a commitment to ongoing maintenance or support. Compatibility and vehicle commands need testing with your model. Zeekr API changes may stop the integration from working.
