About this integration

Homey port based on Fryyyyy / Zeekr EV API and Fryyyyy’s Home Assistant integration. Key tools by Wysie. MIT licenses and source notices are included. Not affiliated with Zeekr or Athom. Shared as-is, without a commitment to maintenance or support.
